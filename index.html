      How the Two-Stack Engine Works
      
          
      Unlike naive snapshot history (which copies the full document on every edit), this engine stores compact action deltas: { type, offset, inserted, deleted }. Reversing an INSERT removes inserted.length characters at offset; reversing a DELETE re-inserts deleted at offset; reversing a REPLACE swaps inserted back to deleted.
      
          
      Click any stack frame in the right panel to inspect its raw delta or time-travel directly to that revision by executing sequential O(1) pops/pushes.
